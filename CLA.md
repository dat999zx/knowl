everything you write. What you give is permission — broad enough that the project can be
maintained, relicensed, and built upon without having to come back and ask you again.

Sign once. It covers every contribution you make to this project, past and future.

(https://github.com/dat999zx/knowl), referred to below as "the Maintainer".

## 1. What counts as a Contribution

A "Contribution" is any work you intentionally submit to Knowl — code, documentation, tests,
configuration, designs, or anything else — through a pull request, patch, issue attachment, or
any other channel.

This agreement covers contributions you have **already made** as well as future ones.

## 2. Copyright licence

You keep your copyright.

You grant the Maintainer a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive, no-charge, royalty-free and
irrevocable licence to reproduce, modify, adapt, publish, distribute, publicly perform and
display your Contribution, and to prepare works derived from it.

That licence includes the right to **sublicense and to release your Contribution, and works
derived from it, under any licence terms** — including licences different from the one Knowl
uses today, and including commercial or proprietary terms.

This is the clause that lets Knowl change its licence later without tracking down every
contributor. It does not take your copyright away, and it does not stop you using your own
work however you like, anywhere else, for any purpose.

## 3. Patent licence

If your Contribution is covered by a patent you own or control, you grant the Maintainer and
everyone who receives Knowl a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive, no-charge, royalty-free and
irrevocable licence to make, use, sell, offer to sell, import and otherwise transfer it.

This applies only to patent claims your Contribution necessarily infringes, either alone or
combined with the parts of Knowl it was submitted against.

If you start patent litigation claiming that Knowl infringes a patent, the patent licences
granted to you under this agreement end on the day you file.

## 4. What you are confirming

By signing, you confirm that:

1. **It is yours to give.** Each Contribution is your original work, or you have the right to
   submit it under this agreement.

2. **Your employer is not an obstacle.** If you wrote any part of a Contribution while employed
   or under contract with someone else, and that agreement gives them rights over what you
   write, then either they have given you permission to contribute it, or they have waived
   those rights, or the Contribution falls outside what they can claim.

   Many employment contracts claim ownership of code written outside work hours and on personal
   equipment. If you are not certain where you stand, please ask before contributing rather
   than after.

3. **Anything that is not yours is labelled.** If a Contribution includes work by someone else,
   or is based on a third-party project, you have identified it — the source, the author, and
   the licence it came under — in the contribution itself or in the pull request.

4. **You will say so if this changes.** If you later discover any of the above was not accurate,
   you will tell the Maintainer promptly.

## 5. No obligations either way

You are not required to support your Contribution or to keep working on it. Contributions are
provided as-is, without warranties of any kind, except for the confirmations in section 4.

The Maintainer is under no obligation to accept, merge, or keep any Contribution.

## 6. This does not make you an employee

Nothing here creates an employment relationship, a partnership, or an entitlement to payment.

## 7. The Maintainer may transfer these rights

The Maintainer may assign this agreement, and the licences you granted under sections 2 and 3,
to a company or other entity that takes over Knowl — most likely a company formed to run it.

This is here so that forming a company later does not mean asking every contributor to sign
again. The rights you granted do not widen when they move; whoever holds them is bound by the
same terms you agreed to.

## How to sign

When you open your first pull request, a bot will comment asking you to agree. Reply on that
thread with exactly this sentence:

```
I have read the Knowl Contributor License Agreement and I agree to it.
```

The bot records your agreement and will not ask again. If it does not pick up your reply,
comment `recheck`.
